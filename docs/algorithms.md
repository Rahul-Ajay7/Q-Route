# Algorithms

## Greedy

Cheapest-insertion construction heuristic building routes one vehicle at a time.

## Simulated Annealing

Metropolis acceptance with geometric cooling, neighborhood operators:
relocate, swap, and 2-opt.

## QUBO Formulation

Binary variables encode customer-vehicle-position assignments. Penalties encode
capacity, time-window, and assignment constraints. Solved with a simulated
QUBO sampler (quantum hardware optional).
